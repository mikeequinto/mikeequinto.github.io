Privacy policy

Updated 19 November 2026 · Personal Assistant Android app

Personal Assistant is an Android app for tasks, calendar events, bills, income and expense plans, contracts and document filing, operated by Mikee Quinto for personal use. Access is restricted to the owner's account. Contact: mikee.quinto@gmail.com. This policy describes the current Android app, not experimental web versions.

Information used by the app

  • Account information: email address, account identifiers, sign-in session credentials and Google authorization tokens.
  • Workspace records: tasks, notes, dates, bill providers, amounts, payment records, invoice numbers, payment references, IBANs when entered or extracted, income and expense plans, contract details, routine names, goals, repeat days, reminder times, daily check-in history, settings and document references.
  • Google Calendar: calendars and event details such as titles, descriptions, locations and times, to display schedules, prepare reminders or make changes you request.
  • Google Drive: accessible folder and file identifiers, names, paths, types and originals you choose to upload or view.
  • Document recognition: selected images or PDFs, locally extracted text and folder suggestions. General-document recognition reads the first five PDF pages; filing uploads the entire approved original.

Purpose and Google permissions

Google identity verifies the authorized owner. Calendar-list and event permissions support choosing calendars, viewing schedules, preparing reminders and creating or updating events on request. Drive per-file permission supports app-created files and folders and files made accessible to the app; it is not unrestricted access to your entire Drive.

Google data is used for these visible app features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Our handling and transfer of Google API data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Transfers are limited to providing requested features through the services below, or disclosures required by law or necessary for security.

Where information is processed and stored

  • On your phone: Android OCR processes selected documents locally. General-document extracted text stays in screen memory until you leave the screen or the upload completes. Camera captures and previews may use app-private temporary cache. Sign-in sessions use Android Keystore-backed encryption. Bill, calendar and routine reminder schedules are stored on the phone for Android notifications.
  • Supabase: the backend handles authentication, workspace records, bill-document references, contract records and their Drive file links, income and expense entries, and encrypted Google authorization tokens. Bill fields saved after local extraction are included in workspace records. The backend performs requested Calendar and Drive operations.
  • Google Drive: originals are stored privately in your Google account after approval. General-document original bytes upload directly from Android to Drive. The filename, size, type, destination and upload setup pass through the backend; the extracted text does not.
  • Bill attachments and previews: these have a separate flow. Bill original bytes pass through the backend during attachment upload, and retrieved bill bytes pass through it for in-app previews. Drive is the original-file storage location; the backend does not intentionally retain an extra original-file copy.

The Android app does not send documents to cloud OCR or cloud AI for extraction or question answering. No document-chat AI is included. Google and Supabase process information necessary for their storage, authentication and hosting services under their own policies, and may retain operational logs or backups.

Sharing, hosting and security

Google and Supabase receive information necessary for the features above. This information website contains no advertising or analytics scripts, sign-in form or document-upload facility. GitHub Pages, when hosting this site, may process visitor connection information under GitHub's privacy statement.

The app uses HTTPS, ownership-based database access controls and encrypted Google token storage. These protections cannot guarantee absolute security. Phone notifications may contain provider or event titles, depending on Android notification settings.

Your controls, retention and deletion

  • Review fields, filenames and destinations before saving or uploading. Manual bill entry is available instead of OCR.
  • Settings lets you disconnect Google, sign out, export workspace records or disable phone reminders. Signing out clears the saved session and cancels scheduled app reminders on that phone.
  • Revoke access at any time in Google Account connections. Revocation does not automatically erase existing records, events or files.
  • Workspace records remain until removed. Deleting a bill through the app, where allowed, also moves its attached original to Drive Trash. Payment entries must be undone before deleting their bill. Archiving a routine stops future reminders and keeps its recorded history. Deleting a contract removes its record and document links, while keeping its original files in Drive. Use Google Drive to manage general filed documents and permanently delete trashed files.
  • Calendar events remain in Google Calendar until removed there. Deleting a local task does not promise deletion of a corresponding Calendar event.
  • Contact mikee.quinto@gmail.com for deletion of remaining account/backend records or questions about data handling. Provider backups, logs or legal retention requirements may delay removal of residual copies; immediate erasure from every backup is not promised.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if data practices change. The date above identifies this version. Send questions or data requests to mikee.quinto@gmail.com.
