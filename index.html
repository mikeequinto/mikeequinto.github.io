CONNECTED WITH YOUR PERMISSION

Why the app requests Google access

Google account identity
Your Google email identifies the account you authorize and checks that it belongs to the app's owner.
Google Calendar
Calendar-list access lets you choose a calendar. Event access lets the app display events, prepare reminders, and create or update events you request.
Google Drive
Per-file access lets the app create folders and upload, view or manage files created with or made accessible to the app. It does not request unrestricted access to every file in your Drive.

You can disconnect Google in the app or revoke access in your Google Account connections. Disconnecting does not by itself delete calendar events or Drive originals.
